GRIDFORGE V2 — CURRENT WORKSPACE / PANEL AUDIT STATUS
======================================================================

Architectural authority
-----------------------

MainWindow
    owns the overall Qt dock-area/layout infrastructure.

Workspace/Layout
    owns the logical arrangement of editors/panels.

WorkspacePlacement
    owns canonical logical placement.

WorkspaceRealizer
    translates Workspace layout decisions into MainWindow operations.

PanelsPlugin
    owns panel composition and panel presentation capabilities.

PanelRegistry
    owns panel registration.

PanelManager
    owns panel runtime lifecycle.

PanelInstance
    owns runtime panel lifecycle.

PanelState
    owns panel lifecycle state only.

PanelDescriptor
    describes panel metadata/capabilities only.

Critical invariant
------------------

PanelSpec / PanelDescriptor / PanelState MUST NOT independently define
the canonical logical Workspace area.

Workspace/Layout is the sole authority for editor/panel arrangement.

GF-AUD-071
======================================================================

File:
    ui/plugins/panels_plugin.py

Status:
    🔴 ARCHITECTURAL CONFLICT — CORRECTION REQUIRED

Confirmed finding:

    PanelSpec currently contains placement/layout concerns.

    PanelsPlugin directly performs Qt docking operations including
    addDockWidget(), tabifyDockWidget(), and removeDockWidget().

This violates the frozen ownership boundary.

Required architecture:

    PanelsPlugin
        → panel composition/capabilities

    WorkspacePlacement
        → placement

    WorkspaceLayout
        → arrangement

    WorkspaceRealizer
        → realization

    MainWindow
        → Qt dock host operations

Required modifications:

    1. PanelSpec.area must not define canonical Workspace placement.

    2. Direct docking realization must move behind WorkspaceRealizer /
       MainWindow.

    3. PanelsPlugin must remain a panel composition plugin.

    4. Panel capabilities such as closable, movable and floatable may
       remain because they describe panel capability, not placement.

    5. PanelSpec.tabbed must not become authoritative layout policy.

Decision:
    DO NOT delete PanelsPlugin.

    Correct its ownership boundary.
